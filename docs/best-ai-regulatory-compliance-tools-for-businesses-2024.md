## Understanding the AI Regulatory Landscape

Before diving into specific tools, it’s essential to understand the regulatory framework these solutions help address:

- EU AI Act requirements and compliance deadlines

- US state-specific AI regulations (e.g., Colorado AI Act)

- Industry-specific guidelines (healthcare, finance, etc.)

- International AI governance frameworks

According to recent research by Gartner, 75% of large enterprises will require specialized AI governance tools by 2025 to maintain compliance with new regulations (Gartner, 2024).

## Legal Framework Compliance

Modern AI compliance tools must address requirements from various regulations:

- **EU AI Act:**

- Article 16 requirements for high-risk AI systems

- Documentation obligations under Article 18

- Risk management systems (Article 9)

- **US Regulations:**

- State-specific requirements (California, Colorado, etc.)

- Industry-specific regulations (HIPAA, FCRA)

- Federal guidelines
