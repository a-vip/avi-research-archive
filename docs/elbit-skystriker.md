# Elbit Skystriker

Skystriker is a Loitering Munition / Unmanned Aerial System (UAS) manufactured by Israeli company Elbit Systems. Revealed in 2017 at the Paris Air Show and confirmed in operation since later that year, the munition can be launched from a variety of ground and aerial platforms.

## Basic Weapon Facts

### I. Background

- **Manufacturer**: Elbit

- **Suppliers**: Unknown

- **Country of origin**: Israel

- **Developmental stage**: Operational

### III. Autonomy

**Autonomous Targeting capabilities:**

The system is advertised as “fully autonomous” and as a “surprise attacker”. It navigates based on an electro-optical “lock” on self-identified targets. It is also claimed to have the capacity to “precisely strike targets while maintaining a ‘man in the loop’ even in GPS and communication denied environments.”
