# X5 Attack Drone

> [!NOTE]
> **Canonical Source**: This dossier was authored by [Avi Perera](https://aviperera.com) and originally published at **[https://aviperera.com/x5-attack-drone/](https://aviperera.com/x5-attack-drone/)**.
> Published on `2026-01-08`.

---

The X5 Attack Drone was showcased at the Zhuhai Airshow in China at the end of 2024. Limited information is available about the drone, though reports indicate that it is capable of autonomously tracking, recognizing, and engaging targets. Two versions of the X5 were showcased: the X5-65 and the X5-85.

*(Credits: Global Defence Agency)*

## Basic Weapon Facts

### I. Background

- **Manufacturer**: Shengong Technology

- **Suppliers**: In collaboration with the Institute of Automa

- **Country of origin**: China

- **Developmental stage**: Approved for production

- **System version**: X5-65 and X5-85

- **System type**: Vertical Loitering Munition / UAV
