# Elbit Seagull USV

Elbit’s Seagull is an Unmanned Surface System (USS) armed with a 12.7mm remote-controlled weapon system, a torpedo launching system and a non-lethal weapon system. It is one of just a few of such systems currently operational in active war zones.
