# The Definitive AI Resources Repository

A curated compilation of artificial intelligence resources, selected to assist with research, governance, education, creativity, and productivity. This repository synthesises perspectives from world-leading institutions alongside the best tools, platforms, and communities the AI ecosystem has to offer, serving researchers, policymakers, and technology professionals alongside general audiences, students, creatives, and the simply curious.

Whether you are governing AI systems, building them, learning about them, or simply using them to enhance your daily life, this page has something for you. Bookmark it. Share it.

## **🏛️AI Governance Resources

### Organisations and Research Institutes

- Centre for the Governance of AI (GovAI)** – [https://www.governance.ai/](https://www.governance.ai/)**Advancing research on the governance and societal impact of artificial intelligence.

- Leverhulme Centre for the Future of Intelligence (LCFI)** – [https://www.lcfi.ac.uk/](https://www.lcfi.ac.uk/)**Interdisciplinary research on the implications of artificial intelligence for humanity.

- Cambridge Centre for the Study of Existential Risk (CSER)** – [https://www.cser.ac.uk/](https://www.cser.ac.uk/)**Researching global catastrophic risks, including those posed by advanced AI, to safeguard humanity’s future.

- Cambridge Centre for AI in Medicine (CCAIM)** – [https://www.ccaim.cam.ac.uk/](https://www.ccaim.cam.ac.uk/)**Focusing on AI applications in healthcare and medical research.

- Center for Human-Compatible AI (CHAI)** – [https://humancompatible.ai/](https://humancompatible.ai/)**Pioneering research on aligning artificial intelligence with human values and interests.

- Future of Life Institute (FLI)** – [https://futureoflife.org/](https://futureoflife.org/)**Multidisciplinary research centre exploring existential risks and transformative technologies.

- Future of Humanity Institute (FHI) – Archive** – [https://www.futureofhumanityinstitute.org/](https://www.futureofhumanityinstitute.org/)**Closed by the University of Oxford in April 2024 after 19 years. The archive preserves its substantial research contributions on AI risk, existential risk, and longtermism. Research in these fields continues at GovAI and other successor organisations.

- Mimir Center for Long-Term Futures Research** — [https://mimirresearch.org/](https://mimirresearch.org/)**Worth reconsidering given FHI’s closure — it’s where some of FHI’s key researchers landed.

- Oxford Internet Institute** – [https://www.oii.ox.ac.uk/](https://www.oii.ox.ac.uk/)**Exploring the societal implications of the internet and digital technologies.

- AI Now Institute** – [https://ainowinstitute.org/](https://ainowinstitute.org/)**Comprehensive research on the social implications of artificial intelligence.

- Institute for Law and AI** – [https://www.lawandai.org/](https://www.lawandai.org/)**Examining the intersection of artificial intelligence and legal systems.

- Ada Lovelace Institute** – [https://www.adalovelaceinstitute.org/](https://www.adalovelaceinstitute.org/)**UK-based independent research institute examining the social and ethical implications of AI and data, with strong policy impact.

- Alan Turing Institute** – [https://www.turing.ac.uk/](https://www.turing.ac.uk/)**The UK’s national institute for data science and artificial intelligence, fostering research across academia, government, and industry.

- Stanford Human-Centered AI Institute (HAI)** – [https://hai.stanford.edu/](https://hai.stanford.edu/)**World-leading interdisciplinary research on human-centred AI, spanning technical, ethical, social, and policy dimensions.

- MIT Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence Laboratory (CSAIL)** – [https://www.csail.mit.edu/](https://www.csail.mit.edu/)**MIT’s flagship AI and computer science research laboratory, producing foundational AI breakthroughs.

- Georgetown Center for Security and Emerging Technology (CSET)** – [https://cset.georgetown.edu/](https://cset.georgetown.edu/)**Research on the security dimensions of emerging technologies, with particular focus on AI and geopolitics.

- Tony Blair Institute for Global Change – AI** – [https://institute.global/insights/tech-and-digitalisation/artificial-intelligence](https://institute.global/insights/tech-and-digitalisation/artificial-intelligence)**Policy-focused analysis on AI’s global governance and economic impact, aimed at governments and policymakers.

- Data & Society Research Institute** – [https://datasociety.net/](https://datasociety.net/)**Independent research addressing the social and cultural issues arising from data-centric technological development.

- AI Index (Stanford HAI)** – [https://aiindex.stanford.edu/](https://aiindex.stanford.edu/)**Annual report tracking AI progress across research, education, economics, ethics, and policy — the definitive reference for AI trends data.

### Policy and Governance Platforms

- Institute for AI Policy and Strategy (IAPS)** – [https://www.iaps.ai/](https://www.iaps.ai/)**Exploring and guiding the transformative potential of advanced AI through rigorous policy research.

- Partnership on AI** – [https://partnershiponai.org/](https://partnershiponai.org/)**Multistakeholder organisation developing best practices for responsible AI development and deployment.

- AI Policy Exchange** – [https://www.aipolicyexchange.org/](https://www.aipolicyexchange.org/)**Global platform for AI policy research and dialogue across governments, academia, and civil society.

- EU AI Office** – [https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/ai-office](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/ai-office)**The European Commission’s central body for implementing the EU AI Act and overseeing general-purpose AI models.

- UNIDIR — Disarmament and AI** – [https://unidir.org/](https://unidir.org/)**The United Nations Institute for Disarmament Research, conducting landmark research on AI in security contexts including autonomous weapons systems.

- Simon Institute for Longterm Governance** — [https://www.simoninstitute.ch/](https://www.simoninstitute.ch/)**Supports international policy processes on transformative technologies.

- Campaign to Stop Killer Robots** – [https://www.stopkillerrobots.org/](https://www.stopkillerrobots.org/)**The leading international coalition of civil society organisations advocating for a preemptive ban on fully autonomous weapons. Publisher of the LAWS Disarmament Toolkit.

- AI Watch (EU Joint Research Centre)** — [https://aiwatch.ec.europa.eu/](https://aiwatch.ec.europa.eu/)**The European Commission’s AI monitoring platform, tracking national AI strategies, investments, and policy developments across EU member states.

### 🏛️ Government AI Safety Institutes

A landmark development in global AI governance: since 2023, governments worldwide have established dedicated AI Safety Institutes to evaluate frontier AI models, develop safety standards, and coordinate international responses to AI risks.

- UK AI Security Institute (AISI)** – [https://www.aisi.gov.uk/](https://www.aisi.gov.uk/)**The world’s first government-backed AI safety institute, established at Bletchley Park in November 2023 and renamed AISI in February 2025. Tests frontier models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, and Meta. Publishes the open-source Inspect evaluation framework. Has tested over 30 of the world’s most advanced models.

- NIST AI Risk Management Framework** – [https://airc.nist.gov/](https://airc.nist.gov/)**The US National Institute of Standards and Technology’s comprehensive AI RMF — the global benchmark for AI risk management, adopted by governments and enterprises worldwide.

- EU AI Act (Full Text)** – [https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/)**The world’s first comprehensive, binding AI regulation, adopted in 2024. Sets risk-based requirements for all AI systems deployed in the EU and affecting EU citizens.

- Japan AISI** – [https://aisi.go.jp/en/](https://aisi.go.jp/en/)**Japan’s AI Safety Institute, established in February 2024. Conducts frontier model evaluations and participates in the international AISI network.

- Bletchley Declaration** – [https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ai-safety-summit-2023-the-bletchley-declaration](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ai-safety-summit-2023-the-bletchley-declaration)**The landmark 2023 international declaration on AI safety signed by 28 countries including the US, China, and UK — a milestone in global AI governance.

## 🛡️AI Safety Resources

### Technical Safety Research

- Alignment Research Center (ARC)** – [https://www.alignmentresearchcenter.org/](https://www.alignmentresearchcenter.org/)**Dedicated to ensuring advanced AI systems are aligned with human interests, including evaluating dangerous capabilities in frontier models prior to deployment.

- Machine Intelligence Research Institute (MIRI)** – [https://intelligence.org/](https://intelligence.org/)**Pioneering research on ensuring beneficial artificial intelligence through mathematical and decision-theoretic approaches.

- EleutherAI** – [https://www.eleuther.ai/](https://www.eleuther.ai/)**Open-source AI research collective focusing on large language models, mechanistic interpretability, and AI safety. Creator of the Pile dataset and GPT-NeoX.

- Center for AI Safety (CAIS)** – [https://www.safe.ai/](https://www.safe.ai/)**Promotes research on reducing societal-scale risks from advanced AI. Behind the widely-cited 2023 AI risk statement signed by leading AI researchers.

- Anthropic Research** – [https://www.anthropic.com/research](https://www.anthropic.com/research)**Anthropic has a leading AI safety team. Its research covers constitutional AI, mechanistic interpretability, alignment science, and responsible scaling. Publishes extensively in peer-reviewed venues.

- Redwood Research** – [https://www.redwoodresearch.org/](https://www.redwoodresearch.org/)**Safety-focused AI lab working on adversarial robustness, interpretability, and dangerous capability evaluations.

- Apollo Research** – [https://www.apolloresearch.ai/](https://www.apolloresearch.ai/)**Independent AI safety organisation specialising in evaluating deceptive and dangerous capabilities in frontier AI models.

- METR (Model Evaluation and Threat Research)** – [https://metr.org/](https://metr.org/)**Conducts rigorous autonomous capability evaluations of frontier AI models. Works directly with leading labs ahead of model deployment.

- Cooperative AI Foundation** – [https://www.cooperativeai.com/](https://www.cooperativeai.com/)**Advances research on making AI systems cooperate effectively with humans and with each other.

### Safety Frameworks and Guides

- OECD AI Principles** – [https://oecd.ai/en/ai-principles](https://oecd.ai/en/ai-principles)**Guiding ethical, human-centred, and accountable AI development globally. The most widely-endorsed international AI principles framework.

- EU Artificial Intelligence Act** – [https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/)**The world’s first comprehensive binding AI regulation. Required reading for anyone operating in AI governance, law, or policy.

- NIST AI Risk Management Framework** – [https://airc.nist.gov/Home](https://airc.nist.gov/Home)**The US government’s authoritative framework for managing AI risks. Widely adopted globally as a governance and compliance standard.

- ISO/IEC 42001:2023** – [https://www.iso.org/standard/81230.html](https://www.iso.org/standard/81230.html)**The international standard for AI management systems — the AI equivalent of ISO 27001. Covers governance, risk management, and responsible AI practices across the full lifecycle.

- ISO/IEC 23894:2023** – [https://www.iso.org/standard/77304.html](https://www.iso.org/standard/77304.html)**Guidance on organisational risk management for AI systems development and deployment.

- Model Card Frameworks** – [https://huggingface.co/docs/hub/model-cards](https://huggingface.co/docs/hub/model-cards)**Tools promoting transparency by documenting AI models’ use cases, limitations, and performance metrics.

- Singapore Model AI Governance Framework** – [https://www.pdpc.gov.sg/help-and-resources/2020/01/model-ai-governance-framework](https://www.pdpc.gov.sg/help-and-resources/2020/01/model-ai-governance-framework)**A framework offering detailed guidance on ethical and governance issues for AI in the private sector. Highly practical and internationally influential.

- Anthropic Responsible Scaling Policy** – [https://www.anthropic.com/responsible-scaling-policy](https://www.anthropic.com/responsible-scaling-policy)**Anthropic’s published commitments on when to pause AI development based on dangerous capability thresholds. A model for the field.

- AISI Inspect Evaluation Framework** – [https://inspect.ai-safety-institute.org.uk/](https://inspect.ai-safety-institute.org.uk/)**The UK government’s open-source AI safety evaluation toolkit, freely available to researchers, companies, and governments worldwide.

- RAISED Framework** – [https://raised.world/](https://raised.world/)**A practical framework for responsible AI development and deployment.

- UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of AI** — [https://www.unesco.org/en/artificial-intelligence/recommendation-ethics](https://www.unesco.org/en/artificial-intelligence/recommendation-ethics)**The first global standard-setting instrument on AI ethics, adopted by all 193 UNESCO member states. Covers human dignity, rights, sustainability, and inclusion across the full AI lifecycle.

- Council of Europe Framework Convention on AI** — [https://www.coe.int/en/web/artificial-intelligence/the-council-of-europe-framework-convention-on-ai](https://www.coe.int/en/web/artificial-intelligence/the-council-of-europe-framework-convention-on-ai)**The first binding international treaty on AI, signed by Council of Europe member states and key partners including the US, UK, and EU. Establishes a human rights framework for AI development and deployment.

## 🛠️Technical AI Resources

### Learning and Development

- AI Safety Fundamentals** – [https://aisafetyfundamentals.com/](https://aisafetyfundamentals.com/)**Free online courses by BlueDot Impact covering AI safety, alignment, and governance for professionals and students.

- DeepLearning.AI** – [https://www.deeplearning.ai/](https://www.deeplearning.ai/)**Andrew Ng’s flagship platform offering dozens of AI specialisations, including the world’s most popular machine learning courses. Many free to audit.

- fast.ai** – [https://www.fast.ai/](https://www.fast.ai/)**Free practical deep learning courses taught from the top down — among the best in the world for applied practitioners. No heavy maths prerequisites required.

- Hugging Face** – [https://huggingface.co/](https://huggingface.co/)**The central hub for open-source machine learning models, datasets, and demos. The GitHub of AI.
