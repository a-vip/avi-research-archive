# P.S. Killer Kamikaze Drone

The P.S. Killer Kamikaze (PSK) was developed by XDOWN, an American defense startup. The unmanned aerial system (UAS) is designed to be hand-thrown and is reportedly capable of becoming fully operational within two seconds.
